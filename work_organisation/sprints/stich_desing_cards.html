Senior Backend Engineer

TechFlow • Remote
payments £85k - £110k

Overview

A senior backend role focused on high-performance systems for a global logistics platform. Strong Python and Django background required, with mentorship responsibilities for junior engineers.

Full Description

We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Core Platform team. You will be responsible for designing, building, and maintaining high-performance, scalable systems that power our global logistics platform. You'll work closely with product managers, frontend engineers, and data scientists to deliver robust solutions.

As a senior member of the team, you will also be expected to provide technical guidance and mentorship to other engineers, ensuring code quality and best practices are maintained.

Key Responsibilities
  • Lead the design and implementation of new backend services using Python and Django.
  • Migrate critical components from a monolithic architecture to microservices.
  • Optimize database queries and system performance to handle increasing traffic loads.
  • Conduct code reviews and provide constructive feedback to team members.
Requirements
  • 5+ years of experience in backend development with Python.
  • Strong knowledge of PostgreSQL and Redis.
  • Experience with cloud platforms, preferably AWS.
